# ARTBIO

> ARTBIO is a clinical-stage radiopharmaceutical company developing targeted Alpha Radioligand Therapies (ARTs) built on the lead-212 (212Pb) isotope, paired with its proprietary AlphaDirect 212Pb isolation and distributed-manufacturing technology. Founded in 2021 out of the University of Oslo and Norway's Radium Hospital by Roy Larsen and Oyvind Bruland — the scientific co-founders behind Xofigo — it operates from Cambridge, Massachusetts and Oslo. ## Pipeline

- AB001 (212Pb-NG001): lead program, Phase 1 first-in-human, metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer.
- ABD: non-clinical, triple-negative breast cancer and head and neck squamous cell carcinoma.
- ABA (212Pb, undisclosed target): non-clinical, non-small cell lung cancer and pancreatic cancer.
- ABC (212Pb, undisclosed target): discovery stage, multiple solid tumors.
- HEARTS: discovery program, multiple solid tumors.
